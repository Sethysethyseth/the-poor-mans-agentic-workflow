# The trial playbook - Level 2, run so the contrast is measured

**The intent, stated openly:** this trial is sequenced to sell you the
second seat. That's honest persuasion, not a dark pattern - you'll make
the call on your own tracker rows, not our enthusiasm, and what's being
sold is the RELAY (a second seat of any capable agent), not one vendor.
The worked example is Cursor; any executor with an agent mode qualifies
at Level 3. And if your numbers say no: Level 1 is a fully supported
destination, not a failure state - nothing gets uninstalled either way.

**The hedge, before anything else (as of July 2026):** executor trial
terms are community-reported and UNSTABLE - reports conflict on length
(two weeks / one week / removed entirely), the pricing page doesn't
state them, and we could not verify when the trial clock starts
(account signup vs first use). Check the executor's pricing page
(worked example: cursor.com/pricing) the week you do this, and treat
everything below as a shape, not a promise. The whole playbook survives
a shorter trial - compress the weeks; keep the order.

## When to pull the trigger (not before)

Signing up early burns the clock during your learning weeks - the exact
contrast the trial exists to show you needs a "before" you've actually
felt. Start the trial when ALL three hold:

1. You've run Level 1 long enough that the loop is habit - several
   clean units through the full relay, no cheat-sheet checking.
2. Your [usage tracker](../templates/usage-tracker.md) rows show the
   single-meter squeeze: executor sessions eating windows you wanted
   for planning, units queued waiting on the meter.
3. A week of REAL queued work is ready to run - the trial measures
   nothing against toy tasks.

Then create the executor account at the START of that work-week.

## Week one - route the heavy units

Change exactly one thing: your token-heaviest real units go to the
executor seat instead of your own cheap sessions. Same blocks, same
dispatch line, same review - the roles-not-tools property means the
swap is the pointer line and nothing else.

Log mini-receipts in the usage tracker as you go (it doubles as the
trial's evidence sheet): units shipped, bounces, review catches,
cap-hits on the planner seat vs your Level 1 weeks, and what the
executor's own usage meter shows spent.

## Week two - taste what one seat can't do

Run one pair of file-disjoint blocks in parallel via the
[worktree ritual](worktree-ritual.md) - the reviewer lands unit N while
the executor builds N+1. This is the thing that is structurally
impossible on a single seat, whatever the meter says. Keep the
preconditions strict; a messy parallel week proves nothing either way.

## Trial's end - read your own rows

Sit down with the tracker and answer three questions:

- Did the single-meter squeeze disappear (planner cap-hits down,
  windows spent on planning instead of execution)?
- Did throughput move (units/week, and the week-two parallel taste)?
- What did the executor's meter actually spend on your real workload -
  does the included pool cover your month?

**Yes, mostly:** pay for the executor seat - that's Level 3, and the
setup delta is "pay; change nothing else"
([docs/setup.md](../docs/setup.md#level-3---the-deep-end-the-documented-two-seat-relay)).

**No:** step back to Level 1. Everything - queue, blocks, state files,
habits - is intact; you keep the protocol and lose nothing but the
second seat you just proved you didn't need. That's the instrument
working, not the workflow failing.

**Never "free forever":** the post-trial free tier is not a viable
executor seat (allowances are a fraction of the paid pool and shrink;
as of July 2026, community-reported). The honest exits from Level 2 are
up to $40 or back to Level 1 - a plan built on staying here is built on
sand.

One more unverified edge, disclosed: whether a TRIAL account includes
the headless-CLI/cheap-tier access that Level 4 needs is unverified as
of 2026-07-16 - which is why the autonomous relay is documented for the
full two-seat stack only ([docs/autonomous.md](../docs/autonomous.md)).
Don't plan a trial around it.
