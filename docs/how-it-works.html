Why two agents

Thinking is expensive and short. Typing is cheap and long. Paying premium rates for both is the thing that costs $200 a month — so don't.

Why it doesn't go wrong

The agent that checks the work is never the agent that did it, and it re-runs the tests itself rather than believing the report.

Why you're in the loop

Handing the task over yourself is the only way to see exactly what the builder was told. That's what makes a bad task debuggable.
