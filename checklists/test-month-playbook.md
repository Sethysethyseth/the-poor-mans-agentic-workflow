# The test-month playbook - Level 2, run so the contrast is measured

**The intent, stated openly:** this paid test month is sequenced to sell you
the second seat. That's honest persuasion, not a dark pattern - you'll make
the call on your own tracker rows, not our enthusiasm, and what's being sold
is the RELAY (a second seat of any capable agent), not one vendor. The worked
example is Cursor; any executor with an agent mode qualifies at Level 3. And
if your numbers say no: canceling and returning to Level 1 is the designed
outcome, not a failure state - nothing gets uninstalled either way.

**The trial-removal fact, dated (as of 2026-07-18):** the free Pro trial has
been removed system-wide - staff-confirmed on the official forum 2026-07-03.
The free Hobby tier is a taste of the editor, NOT a viable executor seat (the
allowances are a fraction of the paid pool). If a trial ever returns, treat
it as a free test month - same playbook, $0 cost instead of $20.

## Picking the right month (before you start)

Don't start the test month during a quiet week or on a toy project - the
whole point is measuring the contrast on REAL work. Start when ALL three
hold:

1. You've run Level 1 long enough that the loop is habit - several clean
   units through the full relay, no cheat-sheet checking.
2. Your [usage tracker](../templates/usage-tracker.md) rows show the
   single-meter squeeze: executor sessions eating windows you wanted for
   planning, units queued waiting on the meter.
3. A month of REAL queued work is ready to run - the test measures nothing
   against toy tasks.

Then subscribe to the executor seat (Cursor Pro, ~$20/mo, cancel anytime)
at the START of that work-month.

## Day one ritual - set the decision gate

**Set a calendar reminder for day 28** with this exact text: *"Test-month
decision gate: read usage-tracker rows, decide continue or cancel."* The
reminder exists so the decision is deliberate, not defaulted - subscription
inertia is how $20/mo becomes $240/yr you never evaluated.

## The test month itself

Change exactly one thing: your token-heaviest real units go to the executor
seat instead of your own cheap sessions. Same blocks, same dispatch line,
same review - the roles-not-tools property means the swap is the pointer
line and nothing else.

Log mini-receipts in the [usage tracker](../templates/usage-tracker.md) as
you go (it doubles as the test month's evidence sheet): units shipped,
bounces, review catches, cap-hits on the planner seat vs your Level 1
weeks, and what the executor's own usage meter shows spent.

**Week two and beyond - taste what one seat can't do:** run one pair of
file-disjoint blocks in parallel via the worktree ritual - the reviewer
lands unit N while the executor builds N+1. This is the thing that is
structurally impossible on a single seat, whatever the meter says. Keep the
preconditions strict; a messy parallel week proves nothing either way.

## Day 28 - the decision gate

When the calendar reminder fires, sit down with the tracker and answer
three questions:

- Did the single-meter squeeze disappear (planner cap-hits down, windows
  spent on planning instead of execution)?
- Did throughput move (units/week, and the parallel taste)?
- What did the executor's meter actually spend on your real workload - does
  the included pool cover your month?

**Yes, mostly:** keep the subscription - that's Level 3, and the setup
delta is "it keeps going"
([docs/setup.md](../docs/setup.md#level-3---the-deep-end-the-documented-two-seat-relay)).

**No:** cancel and step back to Level 1. Everything - queue, blocks, state
files, habits - is intact; you keep the protocol and lose nothing but the
second seat you just proved you didn't need. That's the instrument working,
not the workflow failing.

## Never free forever

As of 2026-07-18, the free Pro trial is removed (staff-confirmed 2026-07-03)
and the free Hobby tier is not a viable executor seat (allowances are a
fraction of the paid pool). The honest exits from Level 2 are up to $40/mo or
back to Level 1 - a plan built on staying at Level 2 indefinitely is built on
sand.

One more edge, disclosed: whether an executor TRIAL (if one ever returns)
includes the headless-CLI/cheap-tier access that Level 4 needs is unverified
- which is why the autonomous relay is documented for the full two-seat stack
only ([docs/autonomous.md](../docs/autonomous.md)).
